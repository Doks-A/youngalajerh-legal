Privacy Policy for Youngalajerh

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Youngalajerh is a Quran-learning application designed to help users read, learn, understand and engage with the Holy Quran.

This Privacy Policy explains how Youngalajerh may handle information when you use our application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Youngalajerh may process:

- Information you voluntarily provide.
- Questions and messages submitted to the AI Assistant.
- App usage and technical information necessary for the application to function.
- Saved preferences and learning-related information, where applicable.

We do not intend to collect unnecessary personal information.

2. AI Assistant

Youngalajerh may use Google's Gemini AI services to provide responses to users' questions and assist with Quran learning.

Information submitted to the AI Assistant may be processed by third-party AI service providers to generate responses. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

The AI Assistant is intended to support learning and understanding. It is not a substitute for qualified religious scholarship.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide and improve app functionality.
- Respond to AI Assistant requests.
- Maintain security and troubleshoot technical issues.
- Improve the learning experience.

4. Third-Party Services

Youngalajerh may rely on third-party services, including Google AI services, to support certain application features.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through the application. However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Youngalajerh is a Quran-learning application that may be used by people of different ages.

We do not intend to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of internet-connected services and AI features.

7. Your Privacy

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, sensitive personal information or other confidential information through the AI Assistant.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Youngalajerh develops. Updated versions will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns regarding Youngalajerh, contact the app developer through the official Youngalajerh communication channels.
